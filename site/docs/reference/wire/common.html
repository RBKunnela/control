common.proto

What common.proto of package guardana.control.v1 puts on the wire: every message with its fields and every enum with its values, as the compiled descriptor declares them. The descriptor carries no comments, so what a field means, when it is set and what a reader does with a number it does not know is on the wire contract.

Rendered from the descriptor compiled from api/proto/guardana/control/v1/common.proto. Rebuild it with make docs-gen; an edit made here does not survive the next run.
