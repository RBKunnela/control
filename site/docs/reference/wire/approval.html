approval.proto

What approval.proto of package guardana.control.v1 puts on the wire: every message with its fields and every enum with its values, as the compiled descriptor declares them. The descriptor carries no comments, so what a field means, when it is set and what a reader does with a number it does not know is on the wire contract.
