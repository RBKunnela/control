Signed reaction

Under approvals.authority: signed an operator of the trust roster signs each reaction document, and no route or lift key exists (ADR-0077). contracts.md gives each format.

Document Format Signed under the act Level
Route reaction-route/v1alpha2 route admin
Stop list header stop list 2.0, header reaction-list-header/2.0 stops operate
Lift reaction-lift/v1alpha2 lift operate

Each body is canonical JSON naming its alg, its keyId, the tenant, the project and file: the absolute, cleaned path of the route file or of the stop list, every link on the way to its directory resolved. A copy of a signed document to another plane of the same tenant and project is refused there for its file. The route names a project_id and a scope of run on each rule, and every stop and covered line of a 2.0 list names the project. The header names a serial and, after a carry, the previous header's digest; a lift names the digest of the header it lifts from, list_head, so a lift made against one list never verifies against a list carried or restored after it.

A plane reads the route, the header and each lift through its operator ports and refuses a document no operator with the act's grant over its tenant and project signed; ports that cannot answer read as operator ports unavailable, never as a refusal or a pass. At every read of the list it asks its ports again, within one poll interval, about the route, the header and one lift of each operator who signed one, so revoking any of them, taking the route's admin grant away, or ports that do not answer in time make the stop state unknown until they answer again; a route refused then reads as a route no operator vouches for. The stop list floor, which stops floor init --tenant --project makes and reaction.stops_floor_dir names, is kept for the tenant the listener serves (listener.principal.tenant_id when set, else tenant_id) and the project. It keeps the highest header serial the plane took, that header's digest, and the length and SHA-256 of the part of that list the plane accepted, held to at every read and raised under the floor's lock each time that part grows. A plane refuses a list below the floor's serial, one at that serial with another head, one above it whose previous is not the floor's head, and, as a broken chain, one at that serial shorter than the part the floor holds or beginning with other bytes. It refuses reaction.route, reaction.stops or the directory of either when its own account may write them, and a route file, stop list or their directory its own account owns; only dev, on one account, reads them. A v1alpha1 route, reaction.public_key and a 1.0 list are refused.

route sign --key, stops init --key and stops lift --key take the operator's key, and stops init, stops lift and stops list take --roster and --root-key in place of --public-key: each judges the route, the list and its lifts on disk through the roster before it signs, and says on standard error what it signed. A carry judges every line of the old list as a plane does; with --stops-floor, the plane's floor directory, it also refuses an old list cut back below the part the plane accepted, and without it says that the floor was not read. react takes --approvals, the plane's approvals directory, and will not run as the account that owns it; the directory is the caller's to name, so this guards against a mistake and does not keep the plane's account out. The console writes no stop and no lift at any setting.

--serial matters only while the floor holds no serial: once it holds one, a new list is taken only as a carry naming the floor's head. When the old list cannot be carried, the plane's account makes a new floor with stops floor init in a new directory and names it in reaction.stops_floor_dir, an operator starts a new list with stops init --key, and react stops again what may still stop. A new floor takes any list, so this is the step that lowers it.

doctor judges the route and the header through the roster file, holds the header to the stop list floor without raising it, and names who signed each. /healthz gives a 2.0 list's header serial under stops.signed; only health.socket names the operators who signed the header and the route.

Limits

  • The plane's own account writes its floor: it can lower or remove the floor, or name a new one, and a list the stop writers' account cut back is then taken without the stops it dropped. The stop writers' account alone cannot cut what the floor holds, and the plane's account alone cannot write the list. A stop appended and cut before a plane read it was never accepted, and no floor holds it.
  • A plane serves a carried list after a restart, as any new list.
