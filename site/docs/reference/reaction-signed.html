Signed reaction

Under approvals.authority: signed an operator of the trust roster signs each reaction document, and no route or lift key exists (ADR-0077). contracts.md gives each format.

Document Format Signed under the act Level
Route reaction-route/v1alpha3 route admin
Stop list header stop list 3.0, header reaction-list-header/3.0 stops operate
Lift reaction-lift/v1alpha3 lift operate

Each body is canonical JSON naming its alg, its keyId, plane_id, the tenant, the project and file: the absolute, cleaned path of the route file or of the stop list, every link on the way to its directory resolved. A plane refuses a body that names another plane_id or none, so a copy to another plane of the same tenant and project is refused for its plane_id, whatever the paths, and for its file where they differ. The route names a project_id and a scope on each rule, and every stop and covered line of a 3.0 list names the project, the scope and the subject. Under scope run the subject is the line's run_id; under agent or principal, below, it is the agent or principal and run_id is the run the finding was about. The header names a serial and, after a carry, the previous header's digest; a lift names the digest of the header it lifts from, list_head, and the scope and subject whose stops it ends, so a lift made against one list never verifies against a list carried or restored after it.

A plane reads the route, the header and each lift through its operator ports and refuses a document no operator with the act's grant over its tenant and project signed; ports that cannot answer read as operator ports unavailable, never as a refusal or a pass. At every read of the list it asks its ports again, within one poll interval, about the route, the header and one lift of each operator who signed one, so revoking any of them, taking the route's admin grant away, or ports that do not answer in time make the stop state unknown until they answer again; a route refused then reads as a route no operator vouches for. The stop list floor, which stops floor init --tenant --project makes and reaction.stops_floor_dir names, is kept for the plane's tenant and project. It keeps the highest header serial the plane took, that header's digest, and the length and SHA-256 of the part of that list the plane accepted, held to at every read and raised under the floor's lock each time that part grows. A plane refuses a list below the floor's serial, one at that serial with another head, one above it whose previous is not the floor's head, and, as a broken chain, one at that serial shorter than the part the floor holds or beginning with other bytes. It refuses reaction.route, reaction.stops, its list or the directory of either when its own account may write them, and a route file, stop list or their directory its own account owns; only dev, on one account, reads them. The stop writers' account owns the list and its directory, mode 2750 in a group of the plane's account alone, since an account that opens the directory can hold the writers' lock: stops init --key writes a new list 0640, which takes that group, react and stops lift append as that account and keep the mode, and the plane reads the list through the group. A v1alpha1 route, reaction.public_key and a 1.0 list are refused. A reaction-route/v1alpha2 route, a 2.0 list and a reaction-lift/v1alpha2 lift, which 0.17 signed without plane_id, are refused at start and at every read, naming route sign or stops init --carry, and raise no floor.

route sign --key, stops init --key and stops lift --key take the operator's key; route sign refuses a v1alpha2 document, naming reaction-route/v1alpha3 and plane_id, and stops init, stops lift and stops list take --roster and --root-key in place of --public-key: each judges the route, the list and its lifts on disk through the roster before it signs, and says on standard error what it signed. A carry judges every line of the old list as a plane does; with --stops-floor, the plane's floor directory, it also refuses an old list cut back below the part the plane accepted, and without it says the floor was not read. Only the floor's owner reads it: on two accounts root copies it for the operator (upgrade a plane). --carry-route may name a v1alpha2 route and --carry its 2.0 list: a carry is the one reader of both, judges them by tenant, project and file, and writes a 3.0 list for the plane the new route names, at the next serial, naming the old head as previous; each carried stop takes scope run and its run as subject. The old list is then read-only, and writers refuse it; its owner can undo that. The operator vouches that the old list is this plane's. Each command judges a route against the plane the route names. react takes --approvals, the plane's approvals directory, and will not run as the account that owns it; the caller names the directory, so this guards against a mistake, not the plane's account. The console writes no stop and no lift.

--serial matters only while the floor holds no serial: once it holds one, a new list is taken only as a carry naming the floor's head. When the old list cannot be carried, the plane's account makes a new floor with stops floor init in a new directory and names it in reaction.stops_floor_dir, an operator starts a new list with stops init --key, and react stops again what may still stop. A new floor takes any list: this step lowers it.

A stop of an agent or a principal

A route rule may carry scope agent or principal for REPEATED_DENIAL version 2 alone, the rule about what the actor did that may stop, and only with expires_seconds; the retry rules at 1 only in --carry-route, a carry keeping their stops only within what the new route grants (ADR-0088). Every reader of a route refuses either scope on any other rule, and either scope without a lifetime (ADR-0083).

react takes the subject from the judged run's record, its agent_id or principal_id, and writes nothing for a record that names none. Such a stop lasts the rule's lifetime from when react wrote it, whatever the run's expiry. A finding is covered only by an active stop of the same scope and subject that lasts as long, whichever run either was about.

A plane matches such a stop against the agent and principal its listener's configuration names, within the listener's tenant, never against the envelope or a run record; a principal stop matches the principal's id alone, never its type. Every opened run of a plane carries the listener's identity, so on one plane a stop of its agent or principal stops the plane: every run, a run opened after the stop, and a call with no run, each AGENT_OR_PRINCIPAL_STOPPED (48, DENY). The tool listing is not stopped. A call already handed out, or being handed out, finishes under a stop of any scope. A call under a stop of its run and of its agent lists both codes, RUN_STOPPED first. An active stop of either scope whose subject the call does not name reads as an unknown stop state, STOP_STATE_UNAVAILABLE, never as no stop; a plane with a stop list refuses to start unless its listener names a tenant, a principal and an agent.

stops lift takes exactly one of --run, --agent and --principal, and the last two only under signed. A lift ends the stops of its scope and subject through its line alone: a lift of a run leaves a stop of its agent standing, and a lift of the agent ends that one.

doctor names each rule that stops an agent or a principal, and each such stop whose agent, principal or tenant is not the listener's, which matches no call. health.socket lists each stop with its scope and subject and, under health schema 3, names the stops that match no call by entry id in unheld_entries and unread_entries.

doctor judges the route and the header through the roster file, holds the header to the stop list floor without raising it, and names who signed each. /healthz gives a 3.0 list's header serial under stops.signed; only health.socket names the operators who signed the header and the route.

Limits

  • The plane's own account writes its floor: it can lower or remove the floor, or name a new one, and a list the stop writers' account cut back is then taken without the stops it dropped. The stop writers' account alone cannot cut what the floor holds, and the plane's account alone cannot write the list. A stop appended and cut before a plane read it was never accepted, and no floor holds it.
  • A plane serves a carried list after a restart, as any new list.
