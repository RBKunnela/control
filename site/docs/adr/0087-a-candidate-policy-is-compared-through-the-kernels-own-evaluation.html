ADR-0087: A candidate policy is compared through the kernel's own evaluation, and what a case cannot show stays unknown

Status: proposed Date: 2026-11-20

Builds on ADR-0012, ADR-0036 and ADR-0035, and delivers backlog B27 as ADR-0050 frames it.

Context

An operator about to ship a new policy wants to know which calls it decides differently from the one in force. policy test and policy explain decide a case under one document. A second evaluator for comparison would drift from the kernel's three-valued logic. Evidence holds a hash of each call's arguments, never the arguments; it holds no run's flow and no answer the decision point would give to a new question. A replay that filled those gaps with defaults would report a guess as a result.

Decision

The command. guardana-control policy compare --current <document> --candidate <document> [--cases <dir>] [--evidence <export>] [--labels <file>] --report <file>, experimental. Both documents are signed in memory with the fixed key policy test uses, as policy explain signs them, and loaded at each case's loaded_at. Every case is decided twice through Kernel.Explain, the evaluation the plane's Decide runs. Nothing else decides.

The cases. A case file is a policy test case with expect optional: its format is checked; it is not compared with any decision; its document must have the current document's canonical digest, or the run is refused naming the file. A request of an evidence export is a case when it holds one ACTION_PROPOSED and one POLICY_DECIDED: the proposal's envelope with arguments {} and their hash recomputed, no flow, no external answer, and the decision's two times. The recorded decision is a cross-check, never the before.

Unknown is a result. A case is unknown, with one reason and no verdict on either side, when it cannot say what the candidate changes: an incomplete request, in an export that is not whole or in one that is; a proposal or decision of a major this build does not read; more than one decision; a decision on another policy (the historic policy is missing); a decision not recorded fresh; a side the kernel could not evaluate, on which no rule ran, or that the kernel denied on its own whatever the rules decide (a refused delegation, two tenants); a side whose policy is stale at the case's time, for every source; a rule left undetermined on either side by a field, the flow or the decision point's answer the case lacks; and, for evidence, an obligation the record does not show the plane applying, or today's kernel on the current policy deciding otherwise than the record. An unknown case is never counted unchanged.

The change. The four actions are ordered: run, run under obligations, hold, block, and the binding (non-advisory) obligations order with them. Further along, or as far binding more, while still binding everything the before side bound, is stricter; a block binds everything. The reverse is looser. Anything that moves both ways, changes an obligation's parameters, or changes only the verdict, the codes or advisory obligations is other, which names no direction and is read row by row.

The report. policy-comparison/v1alpha1, written once to a name that does not exist, marked simulated, naming both policies' ids, versions and digests, each input's provenance, each case's sides, and counts each over its own denominator. Labels (policy-case-labels/v1alpha1) count cases by label and change. The policy document names no author, so a labeller's independence is declared, not verified, and the report says so.

Exit status. 0 when every case was evaluated on both sides and none is unknown, 1 with any unknown, 2 for a refused input or a report not written.

Security / compatibility impact

No wire contract, reason code, digest, kernel or pkg/ surface changes. The command sends no call, reads or consumes no approval, writes no policy, floor or statement and reaches no plane: its key exists only in its own process. Two new formats are versioned and experimental.

Alternatives considered

  • Extending policy test: it passes or fails a case against an expectation; a comparison has no expectation and its unknowns are not failures.
  • The recorded decision as the before: it may come from another build, a setting or an answer the evidence does not hold; agreement is checked instead.
  • Defaults for missing arguments, flow or answers: each would turn an absent fact into a verdict.
  • Shadow evaluation of live traffic on a plane: it needs a plane change and its own record; this compares offline.

Consequences

An operator sees, before signing, which cases a candidate decides more or less strictly, and how many it cannot judge and why. An export with no flow data compares little for a policy that reads flow, and says so. Every number is simulated; none is evidence that a call ran or was blocked.

Validation

  • internal/policycompare: every ordered pair of actions has its change; a case unknown on either side is never unchanged; counts over their own denominators; each evidence reason has its input; the labels reader refuses an unknown member, a duplicate, null, a kind not first, and a label naming no case of the run.
  • cmd/guardana-control: the corpus in testdata/policy/compare/ end to end with literal counts; a case of another document, an existing report, and each bound refused; nothing written but the report; exit 0 and 1.
