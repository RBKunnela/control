ADR-0088: A verdict that compares two events' times is at most suspected

Status: proposed Date: 2026-11-21

Amends ADR-0045 on the deadline, ADR-0047 on the retry rules and ADR-0083 on the rules that may stop an agent or a principal.

Context

DEADLINE_EXCEEDED measures the span between a run's first and last plane events. DENIED_ACTION_RETRIED_ARGUMENTS and DENIED_ACTION_RETRIED_RESOURCE order a retry after a denial. Both read each event's time as the plane stamped it, and both were CONFIRMED when the two events came from one export, on the premise that one export holds one plane's clock.

That premise does not hold. No event and no export line names its plane, a run may span planes (ADR-0082), and an export can hold events that two planes wrote. Two times read from one export may come from two clocks, and nothing in the evidence says whether they do. A confirmed finding is the bar for a stop, so a skewed clock could stop a run, an agent or a principal that did nothing the rule names.

Decision

  • No verdict that orders or measures two events' times is CONFIRMED.
  • DEADLINE_EXCEEDED, DENIED_ACTION_RETRIED_ARGUMENTS and DENIED_ACTION_RETRIED_RESOURCE move to rule version 2. Their row in the rule table may not confirm and may not stop, so each finding is SUSPECTED at most, whatever exports its events came from. The rule's version is part of the finding id, so the same evidence gives a new id.
  • None of the three is a stopping rule at either version. route sign, react and a plane loading its route refuse a route that names one, and the refusal names the rule.
  • The rules that may stop a run are REPEATED_DENIAL version 2, STEP_OUTSIDE_PROCEDURE and RESOURCE_OUTSIDE_RUN. Only REPEATED_DENIAL may stop an agent or a principal.
  • A stop already written under version 1 of the three stays on its list: an upgrade lifts nothing. stops init --carry-route may name a route that names version 1. The carried list keeps such a stop only within what the new route grants now: a rule of it names the stop's procedure (id, version and digest) at the stop's scope, an agent or a principal only for a retry rule's stop, and the stop lasts no longer than that rule's lifetime, or 720 hours for a rule with none. No writer appends a new stop of those versions.
  • A carry that cannot keep a stop is refused, naming the stop and the stops lift that ends it. stops lift takes the route the list was written under even when it names a version 1 of the three, since a lift only ends a stop; the carry then goes through. A reaction-route/v1alpha2 route is the exception: no lift is written under it, so a carry from such a list names a rule of the new route that would keep the stop, or the scope its rule must take when the route names it at another, or a new list under a new stop list floor. A stop of another tenant or project than the new route's is never kept, and its refusal names only a new list. The operator finds the kept stops with stops list and lifts them.
  • DENIED_ACTION_RETRIED_AROUND is unchanged: SUSPECTED at most, and INDETERMINATE while no importer stamps a call's start.

Security / compatibility impact

A stop needs fewer kinds of evidence: a retry or an overrun is reported but stops nothing. A plane started with a route that names one of the three fails at start until the route is signed again without it. Findings of the three rules change id and verdict, so a notifier delivers them again as new records. No reason code, wire contract or digest changes.

Alternatives considered

  • Keep CONFIRMED within one export: rests on the premise this record refutes.
  • Bound the skew between planes and confirm beyond it: no plane publishes its clock's error, and a bound chosen here would be a guess.
  • Retire the three rules: the reports still help an operator; only the stop rests on a time comparison.

Consequences

An operator who relied on a retry or deadline stop has none; REPEATED_DENIAL remains the backstop for repeated denied calls. Each operator with such a route signs it again and carries the list. The way back is a plane identity on every event and export line, under a later contract version, with which a verdict on two events of one plane's clock could confirm again; that is backlog, not decided here.

Validation

internal/supervise gives SUSPECTED for a deadline and a retry inside one export, and its rule table lets none of the three confirm or stop. internal/reaction refuses the three in a route at both versions and keeps a carried stop of version 1 only where a rule of the route names its procedure at its scope, within that rule's lifetime; a carry it cannot keep is refused naming the lift, and the lift under the old route lets the carry through; from a v1alpha2 route it names the rule that keeps the stop, and that rule carries it. route sign, react and the plane's route load each refuse a route naming DENIED_ACTION_RETRIED_ARGUMENTS 1 or DEADLINE_EXCEEDED 1 by name, and react stops nothing for a confirmed version 1 retry already in a findings log.
