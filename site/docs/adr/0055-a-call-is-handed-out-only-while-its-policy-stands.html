ADR-0055: A call is handed out only while the policy it was decided under stands

Status: accepted Date: 2026-10-18

Amends ADR-0012 on when freshness is judged, ADR-0013 and ADR-0027 on what is judged again before a call is handed out, and ADR-0038 on what a newer bundle does to a call already decided.

Context

The kernel judges a bundle's freshness when it decides. The gateway then does more before it hands the call out: it may ask the decision point, look up and consume an approval, flip a journal entry, append POLICY_DECIDED and APPROVAL_DECIDED, and raise a run's state. Each step can wait on a store or a disk. Until now only a pause, a stop, an approval's expiry and an opened run's expiry were judged again at hand-out, so a call decided under a fresh bundle ran even when its bundle had passed its staleness budget, or another bundle had replaced it, by the time it was sent. A retry of a held call decided after a replacement was already held anew, because the approval binding carries the bundle digest; the gap was the window inside one admission.

Decision

A call is handed out only while the policy its decision stood on still stands at the hand-out reading. The kernel judges this, so the gateway holds no rule of its own:

  • Every outcome carries a Basis: the bundle's digest, none where there was no bundle, whether that bundle was fresh then, and whether the fail-closed table let a read run under fail-open reads. Only the kernel makes one; the zero value stands on nothing.
  • Kernel.Recheck(basis, installed, now, floor) answers Current, Stale, Replaced or Unknown, and only Current lets the call go:
    • another digest than the decision's, or a bundle where there was none, is Replaced;
    • the same digest is Current while it is fresh under the kernel's own rule and budget, judged on the installed snapshot, so a renewal of the bundle keeps the call current;
    • a read the table let run stays Current while the bundle is the same one, or there is still none, since it is the same row of the table and its record already says so; a call decided fresh that is stale at hand-out is Stale even where the table would open a read, since its record does not say the setting let it run;
    • a reading the clock step refuses, one earlier than the time the plane verified for the installed bundle, or one earlier than an instant the call already relied on is Unknown.
  • The freshness rule itself lives once, as Snapshot.FreshAt in internal/policy, which the kernel and the policy watcher behind /healthz and the metrics read; the development signer's renewal schedule reads its budget, policy.Budget.
  • The gateway asks at three points, each after its pause, stop, approval and run checks there and before it asks whether the agent left: before a resume consumes its approval, so a moment of staleness spends nothing and the hold stands; before ACTION_STARTED; and after it, for every call, not only a held one or one in an opened run. The installed snapshot is taken before the clock, as the pause and stop states are.
  • A call that does not stand is blocked INDETERMINATE with POLICY_STALE and freshness STALE on the plane's own decision: on its own trail before a consume, as ACTION_BLOCKED before ACTION_STARTED, and as ACTION_FAILED with a BLOCKED result after it, as an expired approval is (ADR-0027).
  • OBSERVE enforces no policy (ADR-0013 applies a mode to the action), so there the call runs, and /healthz and /metrics count it (pipeline_stale_handed_out_total) when its policy let it run when decided, which is what ENFORCE would have withheld at hand-out: the kernel's action was to execute, a read the fail-closed table opened included. A call allowed with an obligation counts even when ENFORCE could not have applied the obligation and would have denied it instead. A pause, a stop and a run's expiry still block it in every mode.

An approval granted under a bundle since replaced is never consumed for an execution under another bundle: a retry decided under the new bundle has another binding and is held anew, and one that reaches the hand-out under the old bundle is withheld.

Security / compatibility impact

The check can only withhold a call the decision let run; it never lets one run that the decision did not. A basis nobody made, a kernel nobody built and a missing snapshot all fail closed. No wire field, reason code number or verdict changes; POLICY_STALE's summary now names a replaced bundle and a reading behind one the call relied on. A replaced bundle now also withholds calls already decided but not yet handed out, which ADR-0038 left running.

Only the policy is judged again: a delegation hop that expires between the decision and the hand-out is not, as before; a resumed hold's retry is decided afresh, which judges the chain at that time.

Holds under a replaced bundle can no longer be resumed, but they count toward the bounds on held requests until they expire and the sweep closes them; a busy run can meet that bound sooner after a replacement. Hand-out blocks and decision-time ones share the POLICY_STALE count in pipeline_blocks_total.

Alternatives considered

  • The gateway compares the digest itself and asks the kernel only for freshness: it would hold the "replaced" rule outside the core, refuse every read the table opened, and add a fourth copy of the freshness rule.
  • Deciding the call again at hand-out: it honours the table exactly, but the trail has room for one POLICY_DECIDED, and it could ask the decision point a second time.
  • Comparing snapshot objects or a holder generation instead of digests: the holder publishes a new snapshot at every confirmation, so every renewal would withhold the calls in flight.
  • Closing holds under a replaced bundle at once in the sweep: it frees their capacity and tells the approver sooner, but adds a policy read and a new close path to the sweep; deferred.
  • Rechecking material calls only: a read would then run where a decision at that instant would block it.

Consequences

An operator who replaces a bundle, or whose renewal stops, sees calls decided just before withheld with POLICY_STALE instead of running; the agent retries and is decided under the bundle installed then. A momentary staleness before a consume costs nothing; after a consume it costs the approval, as a late pause already does (ADR-0019, ADR-0027). Each admission takes the installed snapshot and reads the clock once more, after ACTION_STARTED.

Validation

  • internal/core/recheck_test.go holds Recheck to an independent computation: over the setting, a read and a write, a decision fresh and stale, hand-out readings at, inside and past the budget, and a renewal or none, with the same envelope and bundle, a call is Current exactly when deciding it again at that reading lets it run and either the table opened it the first time or the second decision is fresh. It also covers each standing, a zero basis and a kernel nobody built.
  • internal/gateway/handout_test.go: a call stale at hand-out, stale while ACTION_STARTED is appended, replaced before or while ACTION_STARTED is appended (and a renewal that is not), a reading behind the decision or the verified time, each row of the fail-closed table, OBSERVE, a hold stale before the consume (nothing spent, one run after re-confirmation) and one replaced after it (held anew, not refused as used). Removing any of the three checks, the OBSERVE exemption, the opened-read row or the STALE freshness fails a named test.
