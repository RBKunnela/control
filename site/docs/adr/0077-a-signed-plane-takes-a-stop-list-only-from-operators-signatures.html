ADR-0077: A signed plane takes a stop list only from operators' signatures

Status: accepted Date: 2026-11-07

Amends ADR-0046 under the signed authority of ADR-0073, over the roster of ADR-0071, as ADR-0075 does for the pause file.

Context

Under ADR-0046 a route key signs the route and a lift key each lift, and write access to the stops directory adds any stop the route allows and, across a restart, can start a new list with none. Under approvals.authority: signed that leaves two keys outside the roster and the plane's own account able to replace the list with an empty one. A signature on each line stops a forged lift; it does not stop an older list, or another plane's, being put in place.

Decision

  • Under signed the route is reaction-route/v1alpha2, signed under the route act at level admin; it names a project_id, and each rule a scope of run. The stop list is version 2.0: its header is a DSSE envelope of a reaction-list-header/2.0 body signed under the stops act at level operate, naming a serial and, after a carry, the previous header's digest. A lift is reaction-lift/v1alpha2, signed under the lift act at level operate, and names the digest of the header it lifts from. Every stop and covered line names the project. No route or lift key exists under signed, and reaction.public_key is refused there.
  • Each signed body names its key, its tenant and project and the absolute, cleaned path of the route file or the stop list it was signed for, links on the way to its directory resolved. A plane compares it with its own, so a copy to another plane of the same tenant and project is refused.
  • A plane reads the route, the header and each lift through its operator ports. A signed list is judged again at every poll: the plane asks its ports again, within one poll interval, about the route, the header and one lift of each operator who signed one, since the ports answer by key, and a refusal or no answer in time makes the stop state unknown, never clear. The stop list floor in reaction.stops_floor_dir is a serial floor of the kind the route and pause floors are, kept for the listener's tenant and the project: the header serial the plane took, the header's digest, and the length and SHA-256 of the part of that list the plane accepted, held to at every poll and raised under the floor's lock each time that part grows, so a raise by another reader of the floor is what a list is held to. A list below it, at its serial with another head, above it naming another previous head, or at its serial shorter than that part or beginning with other bytes is refused. A carry given the floor refuses an old list cut back below that part.
  • The plane refuses to start when its own account may write the route file, the stops directory or either's directory, and refuses at the read a route file, stop list or directory that account owns; only a dev plane, one account, reads its own. react will not run as the account that owns the approvals directory it is given, a guard against a mistake, since the caller names that directory. The console writes no stop and no lift.
  • The commands that sign with --key judge what they start from on disk against the roster before they sign, and say on standard error what they signed.

Security / compatibility impact

Breaking under signed: a v1alpha1 route or a 1.0 list is refused, and the plane needs reaction.stops_floor_dir. A 1.0 list is not carried into 2.0; operators start a new list and react stops again what may still stop. Nothing changes under memory or directory. The floor is the plane's to write: its own account can lower or replace it, after which an older list, or a list the stop writers' account cut back, is taken. Neither account alone can: the stop writers cannot cut what the floor holds, and the plane's account cannot write the list. A stop appended and cut before a plane read it was never accepted, and no floor holds it.

Alternatives considered

  • Signing each stop line: react holds no operator key, and a missing line is an absence no line signature covers.
  • A floor of the header alone: the header covers no line after it, so a list cut back to its header across a restart would be taken with none of its stops.

Consequences

A plane raises the floor at its start and at each read that grows the part it accepted; a carried list is served after a restart, as before. When the old list cannot be carried, the plane's account starts a new floor, which is the step that lowers it. react writes lines no operator signs; what it writes is bounded by the route an admin signed and by the stops directory being out of the plane's reach. A lift made against one list never ends a stop of a list carried or restored after it.

Validation

internal/reaction tests each refusal of a signed route, header, stop, covered line and lift, a lift for one head refused under another, and a list accepted before whose route admin, header or lift signer the roster revokes, or whose ports stop answering or answer after the interval; internal/reaction/signedstops tests both vouchers, the floor's chain, a list cut back across a restart and in a carry, a raise by another reader between a read and a raise or before an unchanged read, and a copy between two planes of one project; internal/reaction/stopwrite and internal/reaction/stoplist test the writer's chain and the reader's owner checks; cmd/guardana-gateway and cmd/guardana-control test the start refusals, the floor raised at start, a start over a list cut back, and the commands through a roster.
